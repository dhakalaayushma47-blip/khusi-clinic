Our Medical Services

Quality Healthcare Services for You and Your Family

Pediatric Care

Senior Pediatric Specialist

Consultation, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up care for infants, children, and adolescents, including vaccination guidance and growth monitoring.

General Medicine

General Physician

Comprehensive medical consultation for patients of all ages, including treatment for common illnesses, fever, diabetes, hypertension, and respiratory issues.

Respiratory Care

Nebulizer Therapy

Medical supervision for nebulizer treatment in asthma, COPD, breathing difficulties, allergies, and other respiratory conditions.

Immunization

Tetanus Injection

Safe administration of tetanus injections for injury protection, wound management, and preventive immunization.

Wound Care

Dressing Service

Professional wound cleaning, dressing changes, infection prevention, burn care, ulcer care, and post-surgical wound management.

Procedure Room

Minor Procedure Room

Dedicated room for minor procedures such as wound suturing, abscess drainage, dressing, foreign body removal, and basic outpatient care.

Counselling

Health Consultation

Professional health counselling for disease prevention, nutrition guidance, lifestyle changes, and overall wellness support.

Reproductive Health

Family Planning Counselling

Confidential support on contraception, family planning methods, reproductive health, maternal wellness, and informed choices.

Emergency Care

Wound and Injury Treatment

Immediate treatment for cuts, burns, minor trauma, wound cleaning, infection prevention, and first-aid based management.

Minor Surgery

Suturing

Professional wound suturing with sterilization, local anaesthesia when required, and necessary follow-up care.

Vaccination

Immunization & Vaccination

Routine vaccinations and recommended immunizations for children and adults, administered according to healthcare guidelines.

Vital Checks

Blood Pressure Check

Quick and accurate blood pressure monitoring with professional advice for hypertension management and cardiovascular health.

Emergency Prevention

Animal Bite Injection

Administration of rabies and related post-exposure preventive injections following animal bites, under medical supervision.

Health Screening

Whole Body Checkup

Comprehensive health screening, physical examination, vital sign assessment, and guidance for further investigations if needed.

Diagnostics

Laboratory Services

Basic laboratory testing including blood and urine investigations, sample collection, and reporting under professional supervision.
